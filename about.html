MY STORY

A Little About Me

I'm currently pursuing my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Colorado State University while working as a Senior Quality Assurance Technician at Thermo Fisher Scientific.

My path into computer science hasn't been entirely traditional. With an academic background in science and psychology and professional experience in quality assurance, I've developed an appreciation for analytical thinking, problem-solving, and understanding how complex systems work.

As I continue my studies, I'm building my skills in programming, algorithms, data structures, and software development. I'm particularly interested in how technology can be used to solve practical problems, improve processes, and make existing systems more efficient.
